A Brief Overview

Opening on Kickstarter in June 2013, Undertale made its first appearance in their introductory video and screenshots of sprites and clips from their early development of the game. Consisting of a 5 person team with Toby Fox being the lead creator, lead composer, writer, and main programmer, they were able to receive their funding, generating $51,124, by July 2013 in support of the game!


Undertale, in short, is an rpg 2D pixel art style where the player plays as a fallen human in the underground. As the story progresses, the player meets and plays with main and side characters while progressing their way out of the underground. With fighting being optional to the game, there are multiple routes where fighting and sparing the monsters changes the story and ending you get. In addition, with an engaging story that doesn't take itself too seriously and a lot of fun encounters with the environment, each play through will be different than the last, keeping the player interested in more!
